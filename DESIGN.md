## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-fidelity spatial computing, technical mastery, and bleeding-edge game development capability. Tailored for an elite Unity & XR creative technologist, the atmosphere merges the sterile precision of an advanced game engine viewport with the immersive, glowing depths of a next-generation real-time runtime environment.

### Personality & Tone
- **Atmospheric & Submerged:** A profound sense of spatial depth created through deep obsidian and void-navy layers, punctuated by luminous cyan luminescence.
- **Architectural & Rigorous:** Systematic geometry, refined monospaced technical metadata, and precise coordinate markings reinforce an engineering-grade mindset.
- **Dynamic Tactility:** Subtle refraction, translucent panels, and kinetic edge glow deliver the feeling of handling high-end holographic hardware.

### Design Movement: High-Tech Glassmorphism & Cybernetic Precision
The visual language fuses dark translucent glassmorphism with minimalist cybernetic interfaces. Rather than loud vaporwave nostalgia or chaotic neon noise, the aesthetic remains disciplined, razor-sharp, and production-ready—commanding immediate respect from AAA technical art directors, game studio leadership, and top-tier academic review boards.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high luminance contrast against a dark void, prioritizing visual comfort while elevating interactive focal points.

- **Primary (`#00D9FF`):** Hyper-saturated Electric Cyan. Reserved for active interactive targets, focus rings, progress telemetry, runtime status lights, and critical calls-to-action.
- **Secondary (`#8A9BA8`):** Brushed Titanium Gray. Provides cool, desaturated support for metadata, technical parameters, structural strokes, and secondary body copy.
- **Tertiary (`#00FFB2`):** Quantum Mint. Used sparingly for deployment statuses, frame rate counters, optimization milestones, and completed build indicators.
- **Neutral (`#080B12`):** Void Navy. The fundamental baseline substrate, absorbing light and allowing illuminated elements to float with spatial presence.

### Surface Color Logic
- **Canvas Base:** `#080B12`
- **Surface Void (Level 1):** `#0D111C` with 70% opacity
- **Surface Layer (Level 2):** `#141A29` with 60% opacity
- **Surface Highlight (Level 3):** `#1E2638` with 40% opacity
- **Border Ghost:** `rgba(138, 155, 168, 0.15)`
- **Border Active Glow:** `rgba(0, 217, 255, 0.4)`
- **Text High-Contrast:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Text Muted:** `#8A9BA8`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes an uncompromising technical atmosphere through three purposeful type families:

1. **Space Grotesk (Display & Headlines):** Offers geometric angularity with subtle industrial and sci-fi undertones. Used for major structural headings, hero value propositions, and project titles.
2. **Geist (Body & Narrative):** An ultra-clean, neutral Grotesque rendering project documentation, architectural breakdowns, and design philosophies with pristine legibility on dark surfaces.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Technical Metadata & Telemetry):** Grounds the interface in game engineering realities. Applied to coordinate indices, engine version badges (e.g., `UNITY_6.0_LTS`), performance metrics, and callout tags.

### Micro-Typographic Rules
- All `label-sm` and `label-md` roles must be rendered in uppercase to emphasize the HUD/telemetry visual language.
- Headlines leverage tight letter-spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to mimic polished game title screens and CAD interfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column dynamic grid configured to present complex game media, technical whitepapers, and interactive 3D viewports without clutter.

### Breakpoints & Responsive Behavior
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Full 12-column layout with `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Max container width constrained to `1440px` for optimal viewing distance on ultra-wide developer displays.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Complex dual-column inspector panels collapse into sequential tabbed cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Media blocks prioritize vertical rhythm, while technical specs flow into horizontally scrollable chips.

### Spacing Philosophy
Layout gaps (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) follow a modular 4px/8px rhythm. Structural breathing room around media cards remains intentionally generous to evoke the void of an XR viewport, while technical parameter lists maintain dense, information-rich spacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial depth is conveyed without heavy, muddy shadows. Instead, it relies on backdrop blur, glassmorphism, progressive luminosity, and directional cyan specular highlights.

### Surface Tiers
- **Base Ground:** Raw canvas `#080B12`. Contains subtle radial background meshes or procedural grid coordinates at 5% opacity.
- **Translucent Plate (Elevation 1):** `rgba(13, 17, 28, 0.65)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a 1px perimeter outline of `rgba(138, 155, 168, 0.12)`.
- **Raised Interactive HUD (Elevation 2):** `rgba(20, 26, 41, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, bordered by `rgba(138, 155, 168, 0.22)`.
- **Floating Modal / XR Inspector (Elevation 3):** `rgba(30, 38, 56, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`, bordered by `rgba(0, 217, 255, 0.35)` and anchored with an ambient cyan bloom: `0 12px 40px -10px rgba(0, 217, 255, 0.15)`.

### Cyan Glow System
Hover states and active focal items utilize targeted illumination. Rather than diffuse drop shadows, elements trigger an inset or outline specular glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 217, 255, 0.25)`), creating the physical illusion of light leaking through optical lenses.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a sleek, semi-technical geometry (`roundedness: 1` — base 4px radius). 

### Geometry Principles
- Standard cards, interactive inputs, and video containers use a subtle `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius, rejecting soft bulbous forms in favor of engineered instrumentation.
- Modals, project showcase panels, and featured media blocks employ a maximum corner radius of `0.75rem` (12px).
- Small tags and operational status pills utilize a tighter `2px` to `4px` radius to reflect microchip packaging and industrial CAD readouts.
- Decorative accent elements may feature chamfered 45-degree corner notches (4px cutouts) via CSS clip-paths to highlight spatial computation hardware cues.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Cybernetic CTA:** Solid background of `rgba(0, 217, 255, 0.12)` with a 1px solid border of `#00D9FF`. High-contrast pure white text, uppercase `JetBrains Mono` label (`label-md`). Hover state triggers full `#00D9FF` solid fill with `#080B12` text and an ambient cyan aura (`0 0 24px rgba(0, 217, 255, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Ghost Action:** Transparent background with a `1px` border of `rgba(138, 155, 168, 0.25)`. Text in `#8A9BA8`. On hover, the border brightens to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` with text shifting to `#FFFFFF`.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- **Glassmorphic Viewport Card:** Base `rgba(13, 17, 28, 0.7)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and `1px` border in `rgba(138, 155, 168, 0.14)`.
- **Hover Transition:** Smooth 250ms transition shifting border color to `rgba(0, 217, 255, 0.6)` and an outer glow `0 8px 32px rgba(0, 217, 255, 0.12)`. Header area features a monospaced project index (e.g., `// 01_XR_TELEMETRY`) above the primary `Space Grotesk` title.

### Chips & Metadata Badges
- Constructed with `rgba(0, 217, 255, 0.05)` fill, `1px` border of `rgba(0, 217, 255, 0.25)`, and `JetBrains Mono` text in `#00D9FF`.
- Engine & platform badges (e.g., `OPENXR`, `HLSL`, `COMPUTE_SHADERS`) feature a 4px green or cyan illuminated status dot prefix.

### Lists & Parameter Trees
- Stylized as technical data tables or node hierarchies.
- Borderless rows separated by `1px` hairline dividers in `rgba(138, 155, 168, 0.1)`. Key parameters appear in muted metallic gray (`#8A9BA8`), while runtime values or achievements display in bright white (`#FFFFFF`) or electric cyan (`#00D9FF`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Dark recessed fill (`rgba(8, 11, 18, 0.8)`), border in `rgba(138, 155, 168, 0.2)`. On focus, switches immediately to `1px` solid `#00D9FF` with an inner glow. Placeholder text in `#8A9BA8` at 50% opacity.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square and hexagonal profiles with a `1px` stroke. Checked state produces a solid `#00D9FF` central node.

### XR Specialized Components
- **Frame Rate & Performance Counter:** Monospaced HUD widget pinned to portfolio demo frames showing runtime stats (`FPS: 90 | MS: 11.1 | DRAW CALLS: 142`).
- **Interactive Shader / Viewport Switcher:** Segmented control styled like an engine wireframe toggle, illuminating with cyan glow on selection.
